Who we are

Welcome to Audiline, where we're revolutionizing the way you consume news. In a fast-paced world, staying informed shouldn't be a chore. We deliver concise, relevant, and engaging audio news summaries that fit seamlessly into your daily routine.

Whether you're commuting, working out, or simply taking a break, Audiline provides the day's essential stories in a format that respects your time. Our mission is to cut through the noise and deliver clarity.

Audiline covers 11 categories: Breaking News, India, World, Politics, Business, Startups, Technology, Science, Sports, Entertainment and Automobiles. You choose what matters to you and Audiline builds your briefing from it.
