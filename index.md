Audio News Reimagined. Get your personalized news highlights of the day in just a
few minutes — perfect for listening on the go.

## Everything you need from an audio news app

Audiline turns short, verified news stories into one continuous audio news
briefing — personalized by category and synced across your phone, car and watch.

### Personalized Daily Brief

A rotating audio news mix built from the categories you pick, so every brief is yours.

### Continuous Category Albums

Stories play back-to-back with a chime between them, like a real broadcast — not a
shuffled playlist.

### Search Every Story

Keyword search across everything Audiline has ever published, ranked by matching
phrases.

### Read-Along Mode

A synced on-screen transcript follows the audio, so you can read along or skim back.

### Bookmarks

Save any story and come back to it later, even if you're offline.

### Background & Lock-Screen Playback

Keeps playing with the screen off, with controls on your lock screen, headset and
Bluetooth devices.

### Android Auto & Apple CarPlay

Browse categories and start a full audio news album hands-free from your car's own
screen.

### Wear OS & Apple Watch

A watch remote for your phone's queue — browse, play and skip without touching your
phone.
